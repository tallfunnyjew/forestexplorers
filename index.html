    Our Mission

    Learning Through
    Exploration & Wonder

    🍃

    Forest Explorers is an outdoor, after-school, nature-based play group that aims to build a connection to nature. Our mission is to provide an incredible afterschool experience in nature for neighborhood kids aged 6–10. We do this by engaging in games and sensory play that focus on communication, imagination, creativity, and resilience.

    We're outdoors in the Roger Tillbury Memorial Park forest regardless of the weather, unless dangerous or very rainy. Even then, our indoor activities use art and collected nature to achieve our mission. We get messy, figure things out, and have a lot of fun.

    Our time together not only reinforces the majesty and mystery of nature, but it also lays the foundations for collaboration, individual exploration, and artistic expression of all kinds.

    What We Do

    A Forest Full of
    Adventures & Activities

    🍂
    🧪
    Magical Potions

    Using spices, herbs, and natural ingredients found on our walks, kids craft their own "magical" potions while learning real basics of science and natural chemistry.

    🎨
    Nature Art

    We create beautiful temporary artworks in gorgeous outdoor locations — land art, nature mandalas, watercolors, and more, inspired by the world around us.

    🐛
    Bug Hotels

    Kids design and build cozy homes for their bug friends using sticks, bark, leaves, and other natural materials — learning about ecosystems along the way.

    🔭
    Science Basics

    Hands-on, curiosity-driven science learning rooted in what we find in nature — plants, insects, weather, soil, and the living systems all around us.

    🏗️
    Forest Building

    Using natural materials, kids collaborate to build forts, sculptures, bridges, and structures — developing engineering intuition and teamwork skills.

    🌍
    Exploration Games

    Old-fashioned and powerful learning games designed to deepen observation skills, environmental awareness, and a lifelong love of the outdoors.
