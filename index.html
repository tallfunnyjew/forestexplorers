Our Mission

Learning Through
Exploration & Wonder

🍃

Forest Explorers is an outdoor, after-school, nature-based play group that aims to build a connection to nature. Our mission is to provide an incredible afterschool experience in nature for neighborhood kids aged 6–10. We do this by engaging in games and sensory play that focus on communication, imagination, creativity, and resilience.

We're outdoors in the Roger Tillbury Memorial Park forest regardless of the weather, unless dangerous or very rainy. Even then, our indoor activities use art and collected nature to achieve our mission. We get messy, figure things out, and have a lot of fun.

Our time together not only reinforces the majesty and mystery of nature, but it also lays the foundations for collaboration, individual exploration, and artistic expression of all kinds.

What We Do

A Forest Full of
Adventures & Activities

🍂
🧪
Magical Potions

Using spices, herbs, and natural ingredients found on our walks, kids craft their own "magical" potions while learning real basics of science and natural chemistry.

🎨
Nature Art

We create beautiful temporary artworks in gorgeous outdoor locations — land art, nature mandalas, watercolors, and more, inspired by the world around us.

🐛
Bug Hotels

Kids design and build cozy homes for their bug friends using sticks, bark, leaves, and other natural materials — learning about ecosystems along the way.

🔭
Science Basics

Hands-on, curiosity-driven science learning rooted in what we find in nature — plants, insects, weather, soil, and the living systems all around us.

🏗️
Forest Building

Using natural materials, kids collaborate to build forts, sculptures, bridges, and structures — developing engineering intuition and teamwork skills.

🌍
Exploration Games

Old-fashioned and powerful learning games designed to deepen observation skills, environmental awareness, and a lifelong love of the outdoors.

📍

Our Home Base: Roger Tillbury Memorial Park

We gather at Cedar Mill Elementary and walk together to our 10-acre forest playground just south of Cornell Road — a magical space for exploration of all kinds.

The Details

How It
Works

🍃
🏫
School Pickup

Mary picks up your explorer directly from the Cedar Mill Elementary school office, walks them to our house for a quick snack, and then heads into Roger Tilbury Park through our backyard.

📅
School Year Sessions

We run two weekly 6-week sessions during the school year. The first begins in March; the second in mid-April. Parents can register for one or both. Pickup is at 5pm.

☀️
Summer Sessions

A full week of forest adventures awaits! Summer camp runs daily for one week, 9am–2pm. Parents drop off and pick up their children.

🛡️

Your Child's Safety is Our Priority

Our program carries full liability insurance covering all time spent at our home and in the park — so you can relax while your explorer has the adventure of a lifetime.

Your Guide

Mary Koff

Founder & Facilitator

Mary holds a Master's degree in Environmental Policy from USC, where she developed a deep passion for connecting children with the natural world in meaningful ways.

She spent seven years at TreePeople, teaching outdoor education to children from schools all over the Los Angeles metro area — cultivating hands-on, nature-based learning experiences that leave a lasting impression.

During the pandemic, Mary created and facilitated an outdoor "Tinkergarden" program, a nine-week program for pre-schoolers and their parents to spend time together outside, exploring and playing together.

Mary also runs Lakebound, a business that connects people across the United States to their favorite hometown lakes.
